Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026
Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Stellar Home Lighting, operated by TopWash Tallahassee LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through our website, contact forms, and communications.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you interact with our business.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information when you request a quote, contact us, or use our services:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property or installation address
  • Project details and lighting preferences
  • Project budget information
  • Messages and inquiries submitted to our business
  • Website usage information, including IP address, browser type, and cookies, where applicable

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide project estimates
  • Schedule consultations and installation appointments
  • Communicate regarding your lighting project
  • Send appointment reminders and service updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Send promotional messages when you have provided appropriate consent
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Maintain business records and comply with legal obligations

3. SMS Communications and Consent

When you expressly opt in to receive SMS communications from Stellar Home Lighting, we may send text messages related to your inquiries, estimates, appointments, installations, and customer service.

If you separately consent to marketing messages, we may also send promotional offers, discounts, and service announcements.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to a message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at (850) 296-7028.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing any products or services.

4. Mobile Information Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information sharing with subcontractors or service providers in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party service providers to operate our business, including website hosting, customer relationship management, appointment scheduling, payment processing, and communications.

These providers may access personal information only as reasonably necessary to perform services on our behalf, subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate business recordkeeping obligations.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe instructions provided in those emails.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

9. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor activity, and improve our services.

You can adjust your browser settings to restrict or disable cookies, although doing so may affect certain website features.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our business practices or applicable legal requirements.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
