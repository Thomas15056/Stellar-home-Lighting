FAQ

Questions before launch?

Do permanent lights have to look like Christmas lights?

No. Most homeowners use architectural whites for everyday curb appeal, then switch to colors and animated scenes for holidays or events.

Can I control them from my phone?

Yes. The systems we install are app controlled, allowing you to adjust colors, brightness, scenes, timers, and schedules.

How much does a permanent-lighting project cost?

Pricing varies based on total roofline footage, home height, access, layout complexity, and the amount of the home you want covered. The quote form asks for a budget range so we can recommend a project scope that makes sense.

Do you work throughout Florida?

We are Florida based. Submit your installation address and we will confirm current service availability for your area.

Will the lights be noticeable during the day?

The goal is a low-profile installation that stays tucked close to the roofline so the home looks clean when the lights are off.
